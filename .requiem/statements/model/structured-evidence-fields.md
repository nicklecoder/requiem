Open question: should the commits, pull requests and tickets a decision rests on be structured fields rather than prose in the body? In the field they sat in prose, which left two useful questions unanswerable: which statements cite this pull request, and which statements have no evidence at all. Structured fields would answer both, and requiem already reads Requiem-Id trailers on commits, so one direction of that edge exists. Against: it adds a field whose contents are a foreign namespace requiem cannot validate, and an unvalidated identifier rots as quietly as see_instead did before it was checked. What hangs on it: the statement record, and whether list gains an evidence filter. Revisit when a second project asks for either question.
