Record an audit dismissal as a not_related relationship in the statement graph, reusing the machinery relationships already have. Rejected: 75 of them accumulated in one real corpus, and a graph people read to understand how decisions relate became largely a log of pairs somebody had already glanced at. A dismissal is bookkeeping about the sweep rather than a claim about either decision, so it belongs in a store of its own — where it still excludes the pair from future sweeps and can be taken back.
