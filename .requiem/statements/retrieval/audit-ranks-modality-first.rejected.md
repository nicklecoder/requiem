Rank audit candidates by modality opposition first, on the grounds that a must against a must_not is the one decidable signal the model carries. Rejected on measurement: it pushed 48 unrelated pairs into the top 50 of a real audit, because opposed modality on different subjects is ordinary and the opposition alone says nothing. It survives as a tiebreak and as a flag, which is all that signal is worth.
