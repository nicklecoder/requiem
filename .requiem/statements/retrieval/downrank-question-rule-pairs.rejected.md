Rank lower an audit pair made of a proposed question and the rule it gates in the same namespace, after a field corpus showed that shape in most of 236 dismissals. Rejected: the second corpus did not repeat it. In sbs, question-vs-statement pairs were 7% of 947 compatible verdicts, 20 of them in one namespace, matching proposed questions' 6.5% share of statements. Ordering by a pair classifier already puts likely contradictions first whatever their shape, and a shape rule would bury the one finding the question named: an answer that conflicts with the rule it gates.
