Build the facet index from the distinctive English words of a body, reusing the term extractor the trace search fallback already has. Rejected: that extractor exists to grep a source tree, where the prose of a decision is the only thing to match on, and its output is deliberately fuzzy for that reason. A facet is meant to be an exact key, so indexing session or token as one would match most of an auth corpus and promote pairs sharing nothing but a topic — the very failure the facet index was added to correct.
