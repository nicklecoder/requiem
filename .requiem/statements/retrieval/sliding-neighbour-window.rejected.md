Rank each statement unadjudicated partners and take the nearest k, so that judging a pair lets the next candidate surface and a statement is never left with nothing to offer. Rejected on measurement: it makes the queue unfinishable, because every verdict frees a slot the next-nearest neighbour immediately fills. Fifteen verdicts against this corpus moved the outstanding count from 93 to 92, and a real 255-statement corpus went from 415 to 425 after 97 verdicts. The intent was to maximise recall, and recall is still reachable by raising the depth deliberately, which is a choice the reader makes with a number in front of them rather than an effect the tool produces invisibly.
