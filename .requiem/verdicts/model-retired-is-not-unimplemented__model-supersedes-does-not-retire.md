Two ends of the supersede lifecycle with no claim in common: one governs what list --unreferenced reports about an already-retired statement, the other whether link sets the status in the first place. They interlock without overlapping — because link leaves the target active, --unreferenced still surfaces it, which is how the missing retirement got caught.
